How To Purchase A Course?
  1. Open the Courses page and choose your course.
  2. Click the course price button, such as Rs 499 or Rs 999.
  3. The payment page opens with your selected course and amount.
  4. Pay using the QR code, UPI ID, or Open UPI App button.
  5. Submit your details and payment screenshot from the payment form.
  6. After verification, your Gmail will be added for Google Drive course access.
How To Send Payment Proof?
  1. After payment, take a clear screenshot showing successful payment.
  2. On the payment page, enter your name, Gmail, WhatsApp number, and transaction ID if available.
  3. If your friend gave you a referral code, enter it in the Referral Code field.
  4. Upload the payment screenshot in the Payment Screenshot field.
  5. Click Submit Payment Details and wait for the confirmation message.
  6. If the form does not work, send the screenshot and details on WhatsApp.
How Does Referral Code Work?
  1. You create a unique referral code manually for each person, such as UL1001, UL1002, UL1003.
  2. Give that code to the student who wants to refer friends.
  3. The student shares the code in WhatsApp groups or with friends.
  4. When a friend buys a course, they enter that referral code on the payment page.
  5. After payment verification, you check the code in your Google Sheet and count the referral manually.
  6. Give reward only after confirming the payment is real.
How Can I Access Free Videos?
  1. Open the Home page and click Free Videos in the top menu.
  2. Choose the course category you want to preview.
  3. Click Watch Free Video to open the free topic list.
  4. Select any free topic and watch the sample lesson.
How Can I See All Courses?
  1. Click Courses from the top menu.
  2. Use All, Popular, or New filters to browse course choices.
  3. Click Details to see course topics and course information.
  4. Click the price button to purchase the selected course.
How To Access Purchased Videos?
  1. Click Learning from the top menu.
  2. Select your purchased course from the access video list.
  3. Use the same Gmail you submitted after payment.
  4. If Google Drive asks for access, wait for approval or contact support.
How To Connect With Us?
  1. Use the Support page for help and contact options.
  2. Click WhatsApp For Help on this page for quick support.
  3. You can also use the social icons in the top bar for Facebook and Instagram.
  4. For payment or access issues, share your course name, Gmail, WhatsApp number, and payment screenshot.
Other Common Website Questions
  • Can I watch before purchase? Yes, use the Free Videos section on the Home page.
  • Where do I pay? Open a course and click the price button to go to the payment page.
  • Which Gmail should I submit? Submit the Gmail you will use to open Google Drive videos.
  • When will access start? Access starts after payment verification and Gmail approval.
  • What if I selected the wrong course? Change the course on the payment page before submitting.
  • How do I get a discount? Play the quiz contest and score 16/20 or more for the discount.
  • Is referral code required? No. It is optional. Enter it only if someone referred you.
